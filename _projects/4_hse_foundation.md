## Overview

Before my PhD, I spent 9+ years working in Health, Safety & Environment. This wasn't a previous career I left behind — it's the lens through which I see every research problem I tackle today.

## What HSE Taught Me

- **People interact with environments under stress.** Understanding how anxiety, urgency, and unfamiliarity affect human behavior in spaces is central to both workplace safety and assistive navigation.
- **Prevention isn't enough.** The best safety systems plan for failure — what happens when prevention fails? This principle directly shaped my focus on recovery from disorientation, not just prevention.
- **Systems matter more than instructions.** Telling someone to "be careful" doesn't make them safe. Building environments and tools that actively protect people does. The same applies to accessibility.
- **The most dangerous hazards are the ones you don't see.** In HSE, hidden hazards kill. In accessibility, hidden exclusion isolates. Both require proactive, systematic solutions.

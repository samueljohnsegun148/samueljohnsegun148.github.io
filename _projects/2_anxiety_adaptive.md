## Overview

Traditional navigation systems deliver the same instructions regardless of the user's emotional state. A calm user and a panicking user receive identical guidance — even though their ability to process information is fundamentally different under stress.

This research explores how AI systems can sense user stress in real time and modify their guidance delivery — tone, pace, level of detail, and approach — to match the user's emotional and cognitive state.

## Key Questions

- How can we reliably detect anxiety and stress levels through smartphone-accessible signals?
- How should guidance language, pacing, and detail change as user stress increases?
- What is the relationship between adaptive guidance and successful recovery from disorientation?
- How do we design AI systems that calm rather than escalate user anxiety?

## Connection to Indoor Navigation

This work directly feeds into the assistive indoor navigation project, providing the emotional intelligence layer that makes recovery guidance effective — not just technically accurate, but psychologically appropriate.
